ABOUT US

Customer Service Built on Trust

United Sales Agencies aims to provide helpful information and courteous assistance to customers. Good customer service means listening carefully, understanding customer needs and communicating information clearly.

This website is a student minor project based on customer service activities related to the internship. It demonstrates how a website can organise service information and provide a simple enquiry interface.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I ask about a product?

Use the enquiry form on this website and mention the product and information you need.

Can I enquire about product availability?

Yes. Submit an enquiry with the product details. The agency should confirm availability directly.

What information should I provide?

Include your name, contact details, enquiry type and a short description of your question.

Can I place an order through this website?

No. This is an academic demonstration website. It does not process actual orders or payments.
